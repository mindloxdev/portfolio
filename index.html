Services

The full revenue cycle, handled

Pick one service or hand over the whole cycle. Every task follows a documented workflow.

Insurance Eligibility and Benefits Verification

Coverage, deductibles, copays and plan rules confirmed before the visit, so claims start clean.

Patient Scheduling and Front Desk Support

Appointments, reminders and intake handled with accurate demographics and insurance captured.

Prior Authorization Support

Requests submitted, tracked and followed up so procedures are approved before they happen.

Charge Entry and Clean Claim Submission

Charges entered and scrubbed against payer rules, then submitted fast and correctly the first time.

Payment Posting and Reconciliation

Denial Management and Appeals

Every denial worked to root cause, corrected or appealed, and fed back to prevent repeats.

AR Follow-Up and Aging Cleanup

Unpaid claims followed up by aging bucket, starting with the highest value and oldest balances.

Revenue Cycle Audits

A clear review of where revenue leaks, with a prioritized list of fixes you can act on.
